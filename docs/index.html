Questions?

Frequently asked

How do you decide what to offer?

We look at your home's location, condition, and recent sales of comparable properties nearby. Then we factor in the repairs we'll take on so you don't have to. You'll get a clear, fair number with no obligation to accept.

Are there any fees or commissions?

None. Unlike a traditional sale, there are no agent commissions, listing fees, or closing costs on your end — we cover them. The offer we make is the amount you walk away with.

Do I need to make repairs or clean up?

No. We buy homes exactly as they are. Leave behind anything you don't want, skip the repairs, and let us handle the rest.

How fast can I close?

Because we pay in cash, we can close in as few as 7 days. If you need more time, you pick the date that works for you.

Am I obligated to accept the offer?

Never. Our offer is completely free and no-obligation. If it's not the right fit, there's no cost and no pressure.
